# Test whether Refund Office helps a retailer

This kit is ready to use with a small online retailer's support team. It contains synthetic exercises and blank measurement sheets. No interviews have been conducted and no customer savings have been established.

The product hypothesis is specific: preparing the order, packing evidence, policy checks and a supported amount may reduce the work needed to review a missing-item refund. The person still makes the decision.

## Use the kit

1. Run the [interview](interview-guide.md) before showing the product. Record concrete examples in the [notes template](interview-notes-template.md), using participant codes rather than names.
2. Follow the [comparison protocol](comparison-protocol.md). Use the supplied fictional case packets for manual review and the matching investigated cases in a fresh Refund Office rehearsal for assisted review.
3. Open the [local timing worksheet](study.html). Allocate a participant code and group, start each task when its material is visible, record the recommendation and stop when the participant is ready to explain it. The worksheet cannot access the app or approve a refund.
4. Export the CSV before closing. The [blank results template](results-template.csv) is also usable without the worksheet. Keep interruptions and unfinished tasks in the record.
5. Use the separate [facilitator answer key](facilitator-answer-key.csv) to assess accuracy after the exercise. Review disagreements; a synthetic expected answer is not a substitute for a retailer's real policy.
6. Complete the [evidence and pitch worksheet](pitch-evidence.md). Keep observations, hypotheses and untested claims separate.

The [manual case packets](manual-case-packets.md) contain the complaint, order, packing record, delivery status, supplied historical remedies and policy. They omit the scenario labels and expected results. The worksheet preserves the seeded case order and never sends data anywhere. Its comparison view shows only entered observations and warns that a small convenience sample cannot establish general savings.

## Completion standard

The kit is complete when the interview guide, balanced exercise, source-backed packets, answer key, timing/export tool and pitch worksheet are usable. Customer validation itself remains future work until real participants complete the exercise. Do not fill blank observations with invented customer feedback or estimated improvements.
