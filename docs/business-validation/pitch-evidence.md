# Evidence for the pitch

## What can be said now

Refund Office prepares missing-item refund recommendations for small online retailers. It gathers supplied order and packing evidence, checks the amount and policy, and saves a decision brief for a human reviewer. The demonstration uses synthetic cases. External provider connections remain disabled.

Use the current verification record for measured technical results. Quote its engine, dataset, check count and limitations together. Controlled model responses test the integration contract; they do not measure live model quality. A replay shows saved events, not a new autonomous run. A decision brief is not proof of a payment.

## Thirty-second draft

“Missing-item refunds ask support staff to piece together a complaint, an order and a packing record. Refund Office prepares that investigation and a supported amount, then waits for a person to decide. You can inspect the evidence, see why a case stopped, and keep the decision brief. This demonstration runs on synthetic data with external services disconnected. Our next customer test will measure whether that preparation reduces review work without increasing mistakes.”
