# Retailer interview guide

Allow about 20 minutes. Ask permission to take notes. Use an anonymous participant code. Do not request customer names, contact details, payment identifiers or copies of private orders. A participant may describe a past example without sharing the record.

## Understand the current work first

- Tell me about the last missing-item refund request you handled. What happened from receipt to decision?
- Which screens or records did you open? What did each one tell you?
- Which parts were hard to establish? How did you resolve conflicting information?
- Who could approve the refund, and when did someone else need to review it?
- How do you recognise that a customer has already received a remedy?
- When the facts are incomplete, what do you ask for and where do you keep the reply?
- What record do you need afterwards? Who uses it?
- How many requests of this kind did you handle in your last working week? Is that a measured count or an estimate?
- What would a mistaken recommendation cost you in time or customer trust?

Ask for an example when the answer is broad. Avoid suggesting that their existing process is broken or that AI must be the solution.

## Show one synthetic case

Explain that the current demonstration uses local rules and synthetic records, and that its provider adapters have not been tested with live accounts. Let the participant find the evidence, proposed amount and outstanding decision. Do not coach them unless they are stuck; record any help.

- What would you check before accepting this recommendation?
- Is anything missing, misleading or unnecessary?
- When would you refuse to rely on this result?
- Could you explain the decision to a colleague using the downloaded brief?
- Which person would own this tool and which systems would it need to read?
- How would your business decide whether to trial or buy it? What existing cost or budget would you compare it with?

Only then discuss price. Record the participant's words and any concrete next step. Do not convert polite interest into willingness to pay.

## Close

Ask which part of the workflow should be tested next, and whether they would consider a later trial with authorised, suitably anonymised records. Record the request for a follow-up separately; this kit does not send messages or obtain permission on their behalf.
